# 🤖 AI LeadFlow

### Turn Website Leads Into Customers

AI LeadFlow is a modern lead-management concept designed to help businesses capture, qualify, and respond to website inquiries faster.

It combines AI-powered lead qualification with automated responses and organized lead intelligence to reduce manual sales work.

## ✨ What It Does

AI LeadFlow takes a website inquiry and moves it through an intelligent lead-processing flow:

**Capture → Qualify → Respond → Convert**

- 📥 **Capture** — Website inquiries are captured automatically.
- 🤖 **Qualify** — AI analyzes project requirements, budget, and intent.
- ⚡ **Respond** — A personalized response can be generated automatically.
- 📈 **Convert** — Your team gets the information needed for follow-up.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### 🤖 AI Lead Qualification
Automatically classify inquiries as:

`HOT` · `WARM` · `COLD`

based on business requirements.

### ⚡ Instant Response
Give prospects a professional response instead of making them wait.

### 📊 Lead Intelligence
Organize inquiries with AI-generated summaries and recommended actions.

### 🔄 Automated Workflows
Designed to connect forms, CRM, email, and other business tools into one workflow.

### 📈 Built to Scale
Start with one workflow and expand into a complete AI-powered sales system.
